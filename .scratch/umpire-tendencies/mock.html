The backfill has landed — every number on both cards is now real. Bacchus' ABS figures come out at 3.1 challenges per game, 43.8% overturned; the broadcast graphic this card is modelled on says 3.1 and 44.8%, which is about as close to a confirmation of the extraction as we could hope for. The league average is 53.6% (MLB regular season, 1,725 games). Note Bacchus still reads very hitter friendly here where the broadcast called him pitcher friendly: different metric, unknown methodology on their side. See PRD §2 Band 3.

Two things the zone map exposes, for the implementing engineer. (1) The Area to watch phrase and the map are computed from different partitions and currently disagree: accuracyTendency() picks the top of the four edge-region tallies, where Bacchus is low 70 vs high 61 — a nine-call lead out of 213 missed calls — while the league-relative 3×3 grid flags high-and-outside hardest (+3.5 pts of his miss share over the league baseline), then low-middle, then high-inside. The map drawn above is honest; the phrase above it is a coin flip presented as a finding. Derive both from the same grid, or gate the phrase on a real margin over the runner-up region. Related: the handedness clause must be gated the same way. Bacchus' backfilled split puts low at 33.0% of his misses against lefties and 32.8% against righties — no story at all — while outside runs 14.8% vs 7.7%, which is why the Phase 2 card above says outside to right-handers rather than inheriting Phase 1's region and bolting a hand onto it. (2) The 1.5 tile — now labelled Run impact/gm — is favorMagnitude (unsigned, both directions), while the lean band's +0.46 net R/G is the signed net. Two different "runs per game" in one card will read as an error, so the two labels must stay distinct (net vs impact) and the signed one must keep its sign. Both figures are shown unchanged here on purpose — see design-notes.md.
