The away and home lineup pages rebuilt with the player page's parts: a stamped hero with a first-pitch stamp, ink and tinted tiles for the opposing starter, and one batting order card with a hanging name tab. Tap a batter to open his season line.

Desktop, 740px and wider

Follows the Lineup posted / Not posted toggle above. Stacks like the phone below 740px.

One hero, not four blocks

Masthead, info grid, game notes and the club bar become one stamped card. First pitch moves into the same stamp the player page uses for OVR.

The starter as tiles

The opposing starter's card becomes the ink lead tile plus tinted tiles, so the pitcher reads the way a hitter does on the player page.

Your identity values stay

The hero band uses each club's hand-set header triad and knockout mark. The crest is the lab's tile for the jersey worn: powder blue with the glove for Milwaukee, blue with the white LA for Los Angeles.

Desktop uses the width

The batting order shows AVG, OPS, HR and RBI inline, so nothing needs a tap. The starter and former teammates sit in a sticky left rail. It stacks to the phone layout below 740px.

A scorekeeper's row

Order number, photo, position, bats, jersey, then AVG and OPS. His line against today's starter sits under the name. Nothing here depends on this game's result.

Identity values come straight from the lab stores: each club's tile tint, scale and offset for the jersey it wears in this game, its header bar, accent and ink, and its knockout mark. The Aug 16 page is a played game shown as its lineup page: no score appears, season lines run through Aug 15, and each hitter's line against the starter has that game subtracted out. Pitch mix uses the full-season file. The bullpen board is left off, because the live page shows it only for a slate-current game. Umpire names on the NLCS page are Game 2's crew, shown as placeholders until Game 3's crew posts; the feed has none yet. Mockup only. Lineup order is illustrative because neither club has posted. Names, numbers, 2026 season lines, and career lines against each starter come from the MLB Stats API; the Brewers' "vs Snell" lines match the live page. Score-bearing blocks (season series results, box lines) are left out on purpose and would stay sealed.
