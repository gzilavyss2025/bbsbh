Found while building
  • The release rule holds for a whole game. With release at 60.5 ft minus extension, realFlight's flight time matched the feed's plateTime on all 334 pitches of 824716: median error −0.001 s, worst 0.006 s. One MLB game at one park. MiLB parks without extension are still open (#2568, Not decided 5).
  • A phone stage is tall, so the lens must widen. At 390 × 763 a fixed lens cuts the batter off. The cameras here widen the view to keep 6 ft either side of the plate. Inference: every stage concept needs this rule.
  • A ground ball's totalDistance is not where it went. Canzone's ground out reads 46 ft; the coordinate says the second baseman fielded it 106 ft out. A line drive differs too: Young's single landed at 205 ft and was fielded at 257 ft. The chase uses the coordinate for a ground ball, as the repo's hop model does.
  • Fielder runs and runner speeds are drawn, not tracked. The feed gives where a ball was fielded, not how anyone moved (#2569).
