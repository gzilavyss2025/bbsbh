# Baseball video game HUD reference: source list

All 26 images are 1280x720 JPEGs, checked with `file`. Every image is a YouTube still:
- `maxresdefault.jpg` is the thumbnail the uploader chose.
- `maxres1/2/3.jpg` are frames YouTube took automatically from the video, at about 25%, 50% and 75% of its length.

Each "Page" link goes to the gameplay video the frame comes from. The video titles were checked with YouTube oEmbed. "Pillarboxed" means a 4:3 picture with black bars at the sides. "Letterboxed" means black bars at the top and bottom.

MobyGames, GameSpot, GameFAQs, Giant Bomb, IGN, IGDB and StrategyWiki all blocked automated fetches (403 or a Cloudflare challenge), so their screenshot galleries could not be used. The only press shots that could be reached were three MVP 2004 images on Worthplaying, and they had no HUD. LaunchBox had one 365x281 MVP 2004 capture, which was too small. All of these were dropped.
