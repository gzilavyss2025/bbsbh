# Baseball video game menus: source list

31 images in `research/menus/`. Each one was checked with `file` and looked at before it was described.

The images come from three kinds of source:

- **Video frame (archive.org).** One frame taken with ffmpeg from a video file on archive.org, at the time shown, and saved as a JPEG. These have no image URL of their own, so the "Direct image" column gives the video file URL and the time in seconds.
- **YouTube still.** `maxresdefault.jpg` is the thumbnail the uploader chose. `maxres1/2/3.jpg` are frames YouTube took automatically. Video titles were checked with YouTube oEmbed.
- **Manual scan (archive.org).** A crop of a screenshot printed in the MVP Baseball 2004 PS2 manual. It is a photo of a printed page, so it is grey-toned and soft, and it includes the manual's callout labels.

"Field visible" means you can see the live game scene behind or around the menu.

Not found: the MVP 2004 in-game pause menu and any MVP 2004 in-game sub-menu from a real capture. The 2004 manual has a "Pause menu" heading but no picture of it. Also not found: an in-game camera menu in either series, and MLB 09, MLB 11 and MLB 16 pause menus.

## Sources that did not work

- web.archive.org was blocked: the proxy reset the connection, and WebFetch refused it. Archived GameSpot and IGN galleries could not be used.
- Most YouTube gameplay videos for MVP 2003 and 2004 had no menu in any of their four stills. Over 30 were checked.
