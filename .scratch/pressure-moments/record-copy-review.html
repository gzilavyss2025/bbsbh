At-bat record — copy review

The writer's proposal for the Pressure Moments board, checked against how the page already uses words.

Recommended copy

Batting
Came through 11 of 25 · 9 runs scored

Pitching
Came through 14 of 25 · 8 strikeouts

Fewer than 25 at-bats: the real count, e.g. "Came through 6 of 9".

What changed, and why

ProposedProblemNow
"11 for 25""X for Y" is how readers count hits. Walks, errors and sac flies are in this count, so a reader who knows box scores reads it wrong. The writer's own advice says not to call these hits."11 of 25"
"In the clutch" / "Held on"Two labels for one idea makes the switch look like it measures two different things. "Clutch" is also a loaded stat word."Came through" on both sides: it fits a hitter and a pitcher.
(any "won")Every row's game line already says "won 4-3". A second "won" in the record is exactly the W-L confusion to avoid.No "won" in the record.
"Runs in 9"Next to "9th or later" it reads as "runs in the 9th"."9 runs scored"
"Strikeouts 8"Label-then-number reads like a table header."8 strikeouts"
"9th or later 4 for 9"Nearly every row is the 9th or later, so the split says little; and a third figure crowds a phone line.Cut.

Notes

  • Keep the green/red on the box tied to the same rule as the row tints, so the record and the rows agree at a glance.
  • The (i) text should gain one line: "Came through: a hit, walk, hit-by-pitch, a run scoring or reaching base when batting; the out, with no run, when pitching."
  • Avoid idioms in a translated build: "came through" may need "succeeded in".
