What changed, and why

ProposedProblemNow
"About MLB average" vs "Above MLB"Two nouns for one thing ("MLB average", "MLB"). The reader should see the same yardstick every time."the MLB average" in every version.
"(41%)"A second bracketed percent right under "(42%)" reads as another stat about the club.The percent sits in the sentence: "…average of 41%".
"About""About average" sounds like a shrug, a verdict on the team."Near": a position, not a grade.
Fragments"Below MLB (59%)" is shorthand a new reader has to decode.A short phrase that reads aloud.

Notes
