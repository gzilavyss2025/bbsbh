The league line — critique

Proposed: League 41% · 14th of 30 under Succeeded in 11 of 26 (42%), the box coloured from the comparison, and an optional league mark on the tally strip.

Overall impression

The right idea: a number needs something to stand against. But the line mixes two yardsticks that measure different things, and the strip mark would say something false.

Usability

FindingSeverityFix
The strip mark misleads. The squares are in rank order, not sorted green-then-red. A tick at 41% of the width implies "green should reach here", which no strip ever does.CriticalDrop the mark. The strip shows where successes fell; the text says how many.
Two yardsticks. "41%" is every tense at-bat in baseball; "14th of 30" ranks each club's own top 25. They can disagree, and the reader can't tell which to trust.ModerateKeep one comparison in the line. Rank is the quicker read; the average goes in the (i) text.
"League" is ambiguous. It reads as AL or NL.ModerateSay "MLB".
Pitching flips the baseline. On Pitching the average is 59%, not 41%. A line that kept "41%" would be wrong.ModerateCompute per view; never hard-code.
Small samples. 25 at-bats move about ±10 points by chance; a 1-point lead over average (42 vs 41) is a tie, and a rank makes it look like a placing.ModerateColour the box only past a margin (say 5 points from the MLB rate); inside it, plain ink.

Visual hierarchy

  • First: "11 of 26" — right.
  • Then: the comparison should read as a qualifier of that number, not a second headline: same small label size as "Succeeded in".
  • Order: number → comparison → strip. The comparison belongs on the number's line, not between the number and the strip, so the strip stays attached to what it counts.

Accessibility

  • Box colour must not be the only cue: the words must say "above" or "below".
  • Screen readers should hear one sentence: "Succeeded in 11 of 26, 42%, about MLB average."

Recommended

SUCCEEDED IN  11 OF 26  (42%)
ABOUT MLB AVERAGE (41%)
■■□□■□■■□□□■□□■■□□□■□□□□■■
SUCCEEDED IN  12 OF 25  (48%)
WELL BELOW MLB (59%)
■□■□□■■□□■□□■■□□■■□□□■■□□

Three words do the anchoring: about (within 5 points), above / below (5–10), well above / well below (10+). Green only for above, red only for below, ink for about. Rank moves to the (i) text.

Priority recommendations

  1. Drop the strip mark — it implies an order the strip doesn't have.
  2. One yardstick, in words — "About MLB average (41%)", computed per view.
  3. Colour past a margin only — a 1-point gap on 25 at-bats is a tie.
