Pressure Moments — critique, round 2

The one-sentence rows, the green/red tints and the tally-strip record, checked at desktop and 375 px.

Overall impression

The page now reads like a baseball page instead of a spreadsheet: one sentence per moment, and the tally strip is a real scorebook idea. The biggest opportunity is the sentence itself: in all-caps mono, the names don't stand out from the prose, so a long row is slow to scan.

Visual hierarchy

  • What draws the eye first: the record box. Its heavy stamp and shadow sit inside a card that already has one, so it out-shouts the board it summarises.
  • Reading flow: strip → switch → record → filters → rows. On a phone the record adds about 100 px before row 1; still on the first screen.
  • Emphasis: the large "11 of 26" is right. The box frame is one accessory too many: drop the shadow, or the border, and let the figure and strip carry it.

Accessibility

  • Colour: the tints and ticks are colour-only. The ticks differ by fill weight too (solid vs faded), which helps; the row tints don't, but the sentence itself says the outcome, so nothing is lost.
  • Screen readers: the strip is hidden and the line reads "Succeeded in 11 of 26 (42%)". Good.
  • Tap targets: ▶ 44 px; names inside a sentence are short targets on a phone (inline text).
  • Readability: long all-caps mono sentences are the slowest thing on the page (site-wide caps rule, so the fix is weight, not case).

What works well

  • The sentence rows read like a game story; the citation line keeps the game details quiet.
  • The tally strip shows the count and where on the list the successes fell, in one glance.
  • Batting/Pitching flips the tints and the record, so the whole board answers one question.

Priority recommendations

  1. Make the names findable — batter and pitcher in a heavier weight or heading ink.
  2. Quiet the record's frame — drop its hard shadow; keep the big figure and strip.
  3. Add two lines to the (i) text — ties can make it 26, and each square is one at-bat in rank order.
