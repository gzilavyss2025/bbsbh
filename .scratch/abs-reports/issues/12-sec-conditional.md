The "After a win, after a loss" section on `/abs-challenges`. Depends on the conditional export cut and the page split.

**Design:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DdXwqNvni67o4MrJB3wkgY — artboard "Q7 - After a win, after a loss".

## What it is

Three stacked panels, each a pair of bars on one shared zero-based scale with a visible 0-5-10 rule:

1. **Counted straight** — 8.20 after a win, 7.08 after a loss. Titled so the reader sees the apparent effect
2. **Held equal** — both clubs have called two and hold one — 8.30 against 8.63. The gap closes and tips over
3. **Triple-A, held equal** — 9.51 against 8.91. The same size, the other way

The third panel used to be a sentence carrying two numbers on a surface whose whole method is two bars. Drawing it is what makes the conclusion land without prose.

## Colour

Two shades of one hue, not navy against clay. Clay is this palette's alarm token, and "after a loss" is a neutral category, not a status. Every bar prints its own figure, so identity never rests on the shade.

## The one sentence that stays

Under panel 1, where the wrong number is: a club challenges less after a loss because it is allowed to less, not because it lost its nerve. No bar can carry a causal claim.
