Attendance (/attendance) — critique

Checked live at desktop width against the house rules (src/styles/CLAUDE.md) and the stamped look Pressure Moments now uses.

Overall impression

Strong data and a real point of view ("fill rate measures a town"). But the page speaks a different visual language from Pressure Moments: rounded, soft-shadowed report cards instead of square stamped frames, and it explains itself in paragraphs the house rules say to cut.

Visual hierarchy

  • First: the navy masthead. Right element, but it carries a paragraph, so the title competes with its own deck.
  • Then: four stat tiles of equal weight, then a second heading ("The board") and a second paragraph before the data starts. The board, the page's reason to exist, starts ~520 px down.
  • Emphasis: the eyebrow pill ("On the field / The gate") is template chrome; the title already says where you are.

Accessibility

  • Contrast: white on navy and ink on paper pass. The grey fill bars behind the percentage are low-contrast as a mark but the number carries the value.
  • Tap targets: chips ~44 px, fine.
  • Readability: the all-caps mono paragraphs are the slowest text on the page, another reason to move them behind (i).

What works well

  • Ranking by fill rate is a real editorial choice, stated once in the title area.
  • The by-month mini bars make each row's season shape readable at a glance.
  • The four tiles pick the right four numbers.

Priority recommendations

  1. Cut the paragraphs — one deck line, the rest in an (i).
  2. Move to the stamped frame — square tiles and a stamped board card, matching Pressure Moments.
  3. Segmented sort control, and rename "Swing".
