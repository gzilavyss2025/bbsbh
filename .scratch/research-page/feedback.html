Ask the Play Log: harmonized feedback

Two reviews merged: the design critique (layout) and a baseball researcher's read of the code (what it can answer and how). Tags say where each point came from. Each item ends with the question to settle when we walk through it. Two of the researcher's code claims were spot-checked and hold: exit velocity and launch angle are loaded but have no filter, and "ahead / even / behind" reads only the final count.

Gary's answers (2026-11-18 interview)

Data stream: what the play log can answer

1. Lead facts: a sentence built from any mix of filters won't read well. Maybe three labeled facts (Last · Before that · Times since 2015). Needs a mockup before a decision.

2. Baseline: yes, the league rate for the same result over the same seasons.

3. Small samples: under 50 PAs, skip the percent and say "5 of 20 PAs".

4. Row links: link to the at-bat itself, and to video where the clip rules allow. No citation copy.

5. Statcast filters: all three: exit velocity, pitch speed, launch angle.

6. Final pitch vs every pitch: undecided. Research more first.

7. Counts: split into "Count reached" and "Final count"; 3-2 counts as even.

8. New filters: all four: relief vs starter, pinch hitter / position, times through the order, rookie / debut.

14. Follow a question: filed as issue #3192.

Design stream: how the page is laid out

9–13: Gary wants to spend time designing a better way to lay out the filters and ways of slicing the data before anything is built. The layout, phone order, tab placement, coverage note and wording all belong to that stream, and the new data-stream filters (items 5 and 8) have to fit into it.

A. The answer: make it something you'd quote

1. Frame "last time" as a claimRESEARCHER

The list is newest-first with a per-season count. It never says "first since…" or "4th time since 2015", which is the sentence a broadcaster wants.

Change: a lead sentence above the list: "Last: date, players. Before that: date. N times since 2015."

Size: small. The data is already in the result.

Ask Gary: Is the lead sentence the answer, with the list as proof below it?

2. Give "how often" a league baselineRESEARCHER

"12.3% of 4,100 PAs" can't tell you whether that's unusual.

Change: add the league rate for the same result over the same seasons, and the player's own overall rate when one is picked.

Size: medium (a second count over the same data).

Ask Gary: League only, or league plus the player's own rate?

3. Flag small samplesRESEARCHER

A rate on 20 PAs prints with a decimal and no warning.

Change: "small sample (n=20)" beside the number, and a rounded figure.

Size: small.

Ask Gary: What counts as small: under 50 PAs?

4. Link each row to the at-bat, and let you cite itRESEARCHER

Rows open the box score only.

Change: link to that at-bat in the play-by-play; video where the clip policy allows; a "copy" that gives date, game, inning, count and source.

Size: medium. Video has terms-of-use limits already on file.

Ask Gary: Copy as a citation line, or as a sentence ready to read on air?

B. The questions: let it ask what people actually ask

5. Add exit velocity, launch angle and pitch speed filtersRESEARCHER

The data already loads them, but there's no filter, so "105+ and an out" or "homers off 100+ fastballs" can't be asked. Show EV and LA in the results too.

Size: medium. Game mode already has range cards to copy.

Ask Gary: Which first: exit velo, launch angle, or pitch speed?

6. Say "final pitch" where it means final pitchRESEARCHER

"Sinker" and location read the pitch that ended the at-bat. A sinker thrown earlier in it doesn't count, and nothing counts single pitches (whiffs, called strikes).

Change now: rename to "Final pitch type" and "Final pitch location". Later: a pitch-by-pitch mode.

Size: rename is tiny; a pitch mode is large.

Ask Gary: Rename now and park the pitch mode, or plan the pitch mode?

7. One rule per count cardRESEARCHER

"0-2" matches any at-bat that passed through it; "Batter ahead" reads only the final count; 3-2 is in no group.

Change: split into "Count reached" and "Final count", and put 3-2 in "Even".

Size: small.

Ask Gary: Agree 3-2 counts as even?

8. Missing everyday filtersRESEARCHER

Position, pinch hitter, starter vs reliever, rookie or debut, catcher, umpire, times through the order, specific dates.

Size: varies. Not confirmed which the ask box already understands.

Ask Gary: Pick your top two.
