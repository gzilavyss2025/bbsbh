# Ask the Play Log (/research): design critique, 2026-11-18

Saved from a live session with Gary. Screenshot: desktop width, empty state.

## Findings
- Two ways in (question box on the right, five filters on the left) with no stated link between them. Critical.
- Phone: the filter column stacks first, pushing the question box and "Try one" below the fold. Critical.
- Plays / Games / Streaks tabs sit after the question box but change what kind of question it is.
- "Add a filter: Choose…" hides most filters behind a menu label that says nothing.
- "Anyone" (Batter, Pitcher) vs "Any" (club selects).
- Title sits over the filter column, not the box it names; reading order zig-zags.
- Helper lines ("For example: …" and "Build your own: start with a filter on the left") repeat "Try one"; house rule says cut helper text.
- Data line (8,269,040 pitches, through Oct 28 2026, Baseball Savant) floats between tabs and card.
- Ask button may be under 44 px wide; placeholder and labels may be light on paper (check-contrast).

## Works
- "Try one", grouped by kind of question (Last time, How often, Who most, What happens).
- Stamped card and dark tabs match the newer pages.
- Stating coverage and the through-date builds trust.

## Recommended
1. One way in: full-width question box first, tabs above it, filters folded under it as editable chips that show what the question became.
2. Phone order: question, "Try one", filters behind a button.
3. Cut both instruction lines; move the data line to the foot of results.
