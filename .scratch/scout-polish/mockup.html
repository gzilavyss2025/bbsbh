Edge tab · answers Q1

Lead with a verdict, then show the ledger

Today the top of the page is one figure: Expected xwOBA (est.) .332 · League .308 · 72% of pitches. It does not say who that number favors, and "72% of pitches" is not explained. The Edge tab says it in words first, then shows the per-pitch evidence.

  • Two lenses, kept apart. By pitch type: his result on each of Pivetta's pitches against the league's result on that pitch. By location: the page's existing expected value, which weighs each region by where Pivetta throws.
  • The ledger is one row per pill. The tick is the league. The dot is Chourio. The bar under the row is Pivetta's usage. Tap a row: the pill, the scene, and the Zones maps follow.
  • The words come from the existing bands. "Even" is inside ±.030 xwOBA (±4 pts for whiff and swing). "Slight" is to ±.070 (±10 pts). "Clear" is past it. These are the colour steps in lib/scout/metrics.js, so the words and the map colours agree.
  • "72% of pitches" becomes a reason. The cutter (44 pitches to righties) and curve (24) are under the 50-pitch map floor, so the location lens leaves them out. The page should name them.

VerifiedAll figures come from the app's own pure functions (pitcherBoard, hitterSide) run on the 2026 stores in the repo on Oct 5. The per-type values (typeVal, leagueFlat) already exist in hitterBoard.js. This needs no new data.

Reverses a choiceMapParts.jsx says the answer strip is "a figure, never a sentence." A verdict line changes that. It needs your approval.

InferencePivetta threw only 263 pitches to righties in the 2026 store. The page should show the sample size next to the verdict (the chip at the top does this).

Zones tab · today's maps, clearer

Same two maps, plainer captions

  • Nothing is removed. The two 13-region maps, the tap readout, the metric switch, Hand and Scope all stay. Hand, Scope and Metric move into one sheet behind the chip at the top. The chip shows the current choices.
  • Captions say what the map means. "Where Pivetta throws to righties" and "Where Chourio does damage", in place of "Pitcher · location %" and "Hitter · xwOBA (est.)".
  • A callout joins the two maps. It adds up Pivetta's share in Chourio's hottest regions and in his coldest regions. Today you must compare 26 cells by eye.
  • The View control moves onto the scene. The scene is now the thing it changes first, and it plays in both views (Q3).

VerifiedRegion values and colour steps come from hitterSide output for this pair. Chourio's middle and middle-inside cells read .604 and .602. Pivetta puts 8% there.

Meetings tab · answers Q2

Every pitch, and each one opens like the Express Lane

  • The data is already on the wire. The page's Savant request returns every pitch row. plateAppearances() then keeps only the last pitch of each plate appearance. Keep all the rows: type, mph, spin, count, call, location and the flight terms (vx0 … az).
  • The modal draws the real flight from those terms. The scene above uses league-average shapes, so this is the only place on the page that shows his actual pitches.
  • Film. The game feed gives a playId for each pitch. The Express Lane resolver (clipIndex.js) turns a playId into an mp4. The modal would play it inline. This mockup can only link out, because the artifact frame blocks MLB video.
  • The mix line compares what he saw in the meeting with Pivetta's 2026 mix to righties.

VerifiedSavant returned 12 pitch rows for this pair. The game feed for gamePk 776222 has a playId on all 12 pitches.

InferenceheadToHead.js warns: "Do not plot these pitches. Savant's 2026 plate_z frame differs from the feed's." The modal plots each pitch against the zone top and bottom from its own Savant row, never on the season maps. Check this before you build it.

InferenceThe date cutoff still holds back every game on or after today. A playId lookup reads the feed of a final, past game. That reader still needs its own spoiler classification in spoiler-manifest.json.

Scene · answers Q3

The animation, in both views

Today the scene shows only in the Hitter's view. ADR-0093 (decision 5) explains why: its camera stands behind the plate, so in the Pitcher's view its arcs would break the other way from the maps.

The mockup draws the Now Pitching card's own stage (sky, grass, the mound, the plate dirt and the zone box) in both views. The fix is a second camera, not a flipped picture. The mockup's Pitcher's view uses a centre-field camera: high, far back and with a long lens, like the telecast. The arcs then break the same way as the maps below them. Toggle the view on the scene to compare.

ADR changeThis amends ADR-0093 decision 5. scene.js needs a second projection and a test that a pitch to the first-base side lands on the same side as the map in each view.

InferenceThe scene's arcs are league-average shapes for each pitch type at his speed (the MOVE table). The caption should say "typical shape".

Navigation

Three ways to structure the page

All three keep every feature. They differ in what you see first and how much you tap.

Recommended · the mockup

A. Verdict first, three tabs

Header · sample chip · filters ▾ ┌ scene (both views) ──────┐ └──────────────────────────┘ (All)(Fastball 49%)(Sweeper 23%)… EDGE │ ZONES │ MEETINGS verdict + ledger ─ or ─ two maps + callout ─ or ─ PA cards → pitch modal
  • The scene and pills stay fixed. Only the bottom half changes.
  • Each tab answers one question.
  • Cost: Meetings is one tap away. Today it is at the bottom of the scroll.

B. One scroll, in question order

Header · filters ▾ 1 Who has the edge verdict · ledger 2 Where scene · pills (sticky) two maps · callout 3 When they met PA cards → pitch modal
  • No tabs. Section heads are the questions.
  • The pills stick to the top while the maps are on screen.
  • Cost: a long page on a phone. The scene sits below the fold.

C. One card per pitch

Header · verdict line ◀ FASTBALL · 49% · 93.9 ▶ scene: this pitch only ledger line: .391 vs .330 two mini maps in their meeting: 7 seen ◀ swipe to SWEEPER ▶
  • Reads like a scouting report: "here is what is coming".
  • The h2h pitches sort under each pitch type.
  • Cost: the All-pitches view and the totals need their own card.

Smaller polish, any structure

  • One filter sheet. Today five controls sit in three places (View and Metric above the maps, Hand and Scope below them). One chip opens one sheet.
  • Show the sample. "263 pitches to righties" near the top. The thin-sample hatch now appears only on the maps.
  • Name the left-out pitches. Replace "72% of pitches" with "Leaves out cutter and curve: too few to righties".
  • Contact quality on the meetings line. 0-for-3 hides two hard lineouts (105.2 and 100.8 mph). Show average exit velocity and xwOBA on contact (.543).
  • Default Hand to the pitcher's hand. For a one-sided hitter the hitter map pools both hands today. "vs RHP" is the matchup. This changes behaviour, so it is your call.
  • Label the scope split. The maps are 2026. The meetings are every season. Say so on each.
