# SectionHead and Card — the proposed API (#1113)

A proposal for Gary to sign off before anyone builds. Every count comes from
`census.md` (generated by `census.mjs` on `ffd91507a`). Where the proposal needs a
decision, it points at `decisions.md`.

It follows the shape of the three components that already exist:
`ui/control/Button.jsx`, `Door.jsx` and `Pill.jsx`. Each has one CSS file in
`styles/system/`, one class helper in `lib/design/`, a small set of props, and no
"tone" prop. These two follow the same pattern.

## Part 1 — `SectionHead`

`SectionHead` comes first, because `Card`'s head IS a `SectionHead`.

### File layout

- `src/components/ui/frame/SectionHead.jsx`
- `src/styles/system/section-head.css`
- `src/lib/design/sectionHeadClass.js` (class helper, unit-tested like `pillClass.js`)

`src/components/ui/` holds 11 files and the cap is 12. `SectionHead` and `Card`
together would make 13, so both go in a new subdirectory, `ui/frame/`, the way the
three controls went into `ui/control/` (ADR-0038). `src/styles/` sits at its 116-file
budget, so the CSS goes in `src/styles/system/`, which holds 3 files today.

### Props

```jsx
<SectionHead
  look="band"            // 'label' (default) | 'band' | 'rule'
  as="h3"                // the heading element; default 'h3'. 'span' inside a card that already has a heading.
  note="rank of 30"      // optional: the right-hand second line (today's <em>, __sub, __lede, __note)
  action={<Door href=…>See all</Door>}  // optional: ONE Door, or ONE Pill role="control"
  mark={<TeamLogo variant="mono" …/>}   // band only: the club mark at the far right (SectionMasthead's `logo`)
>
  Batting order
</SectionHead>
```

| prop | values | rows it covers | what it replaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `look="band"` | the club band: `--bar-fill` ground, a 3px `--bar-accent` underline, `--bar-text` ink | **22** head rules (11 undressed navy + kraft bands, 6 themed copies of them, 5 club-only bands), across 16 `SectionMasthead` call sites, 15 `.thub-card__head` call sites and 9 `.section__title--bar` call sites | `.metricbar`, `SectionMasthead`, `.statbox__title`, `.dueup__title`, `.lineupcard__title`, `.halfdefense__title`, `.abs__title`, `.roster__toggle`, `.winprob__head`, `.marginnotes__title`, `.pitchers__title`, `.lineupteam__name`, the hub's four themed heads, `.section__title--bar` |
| `look="label"` | display caps, `--fs-label`, `--text-caption`, a hairline under | **22** head rules; 23 `SectionTitle` call sites | `SectionTitle` / `.section__title`, `.thub-card__head`, `.txcard__head`, `.tstats-card__head`, `.roster-super__head`, a card's name in `--fs-h3` inside the card (`.prospectcard__title`, `.levelprog__title`, …) |
| `look="rule"` | the label, then a pencil rule that runs to the right edge | **1** found by shape (`.bcast-sec__title`); the critique's "STATCAST ——— PERCENTILE RANK" is a second one, drawn some other way — find it by eye in slice H2 | — |
| `note` | any short text | 12 of 23 `SectionTitle` calls pass a note today; 5 of 15 `.thub-card__head` calls carry an `<em>`; 43 second-line classes exist | `<em>`, `__sub`, `__lede`, `__kicker`, `__eyebrow` |
| `action` | one `Door` or one `Pill role="control"` | 3 `SectionTitle` calls pass an action; 3 `.thub-card__head` blocks hold a control; the "Postseason odds" pill rides the standings band; `MatchupNotesToggle` and `InfoPopover` ride `SectionMasthead` | `SectionTitle`'s `action`, `SectionMasthead`'s `children` |
| `mark` | a `TeamLogo` (mono art, ADR-0031) | 5 of 16 `SectionMasthead` calls pass `logo` | `SectionMasthead`'s `logo` |
| `as` | `h2` `h3` `h4` `span` | — | `SectionMasthead`'s `as` |

### What `section-head.css` owns

- The three looks: ground, underline, type (face, size, caps, tracking), padding,
  and the flex row that puts the title left and the note, action and mark right.
- The band's theme hook. The band reads `--bar-fill`, `--bar-accent` and `--bar-text`
  from the NEAREST ancestor that sets them (`lib/headerTheme.js`). `SectionHead`
  takes **no** theme prop. A club may colour a card that names the club; it may never
  colour a control (ADR-0030). Theming stays on the card or page root, as it is now.
- **The one kraft fallback.** The unthemed band's underline is
  `var(--bar-accent, var(--seal))`. ADR-0083 allows kraft here (clause 4: "the club
  band's 3px accent underline"). Today `scripts/check-seal-scope.mjs` allowlists
  19 band entries in 8 partials for that fallback (18 selectors; the band mock is
  listed in two files). After H1 the fallback
  lives in ONE selector in `system/section-head.css`. The allowlist loses those
  entries and gains that one, in the same PR (the way `.pill--seal` did).
- Corners follow the parent. Inside a `Card`, the card clips the band
  (`overflow: hidden`, the `.thub-card` trick). On the player page the band bleeds
  to the page edge with square corners, as `.section__title--bar` does now.

What it does NOT own: the margin above or below a head (the parent's spacing,
#1180), and anything about what the head's text says.

### Two band faces that exist today — decision needed

Unthemed, the band has two faces now:

- **navy + kraft** — `.metricbar`, the innings and HUD bands (11 rules). With no
  club colour, you still see a navy band.
- **plain label** — the team hub's four heads and the player page's bar
  (5 rules). With no club colour, you see a plain caps label; the band appears only
  when a club colour arrives.

`decisions.md` Q2 asks Gary which one an unthemed band should be. The recommendation
keeps both, as a prop on the band: `look="band"` (navy + kraft) and
`look="band" club` (a label until a club colour arrives). That costs one boolean and
changes nothing on screen.

### Retired, as the issue says

**The kicker over a title.** "PLAYER COMPENSATION" over "CONTRACT" becomes a head
"Contract" with "Player compensation" as its `note`, or the kicker goes. The
ADR-0084 ledger already names the target class for each of the 39 second-line rows.
One of them, `.betweeninnings__eyebrow`, only repeats its block's name and is
deleted, not renamed.

### Not a section head (left alone)

13 page and modal titles (`.topbar__title`, `.sheet__title`, `.boxscore__title`,
`.mytally__title`, …) name a page, not a section. Modals stay bespoke (ADR-0037).
The TeamInfo club-name bar (`.teaminfo__head`) changes its shape when themed and
carries Away/Home and a button; it is a page masthead, so it stays its own, but its
band colours must match H1's.

## Part 2 — `Card`

### File layout

- `src/components/ui/frame/Card.jsx`
- `src/styles/system/card.css`
- `src/lib/design/cardClass.js`

### Props

```jsx
<Card
  frame="sheet"           // 'sheet' (default) | 'ledger'
  head={<SectionHead look="band">Roster</SectionHead>}   // optional; no head = no head
  body="flush"            // 'padded' (default) | 'flush'
  as="section"            // 'section' (default) | 'div' | 'article' | 'li' | 'aside' | 'a' | 'button'
  className="chal"        // the block's NAMESPACE, for its inner parts (the thub-card chalcard pattern)
  accent="--offday-accent"  // interactive cards only: the colour token the hover tint mixes in
>
  …
</Card>
```

### The three axes, with the census counts

| axis | value | what it draws | cards (of 111) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `frame` | `sheet` | `--radius-md`, `--shadow-card`, hairline `--border-rule`, `--surface-card` | **86** |
| | `ledger` | `--radius-sm`, no shadow, same edge and ground | **25** |
| `head` | none | no head row | **48** |
| | `SectionHead look="label"` inside | | **26** |
| | `SectionHead look="band"` inside | | **24** (the hub's are "label until themed") |
| | a head ABOVE the card, outside it | the player page's bleed band, or a `SectionTitle` over a boxed list | **13** (8 label, 5 band) — these cards take `head` = none, and the head stays a standalone `SectionHead` |
| `body` | `padded` | `.card__body` with `var(--space-3) var(--space-4) var(--space-4)` (today's `.thub-card__body`) | **66** |
| | `flush` | no padding: the table, list or grid owns its own row rules | **45** |
| `as` (a state, not an axis) | `a` / `button` | the whole card is the tap target: hover tint, `--focus-ring`, pointer | **9** (`.offdaycard`, `.thub-affiliate`, `.seedcard`, `.simlike__link`, `.trrank__tile`, `.betweeninnings`, two on /first-scorebook, one on /logbook/stats) |

**How the frame is chosen: the radius decides.** The CSS draws four frames today:

| drawn today | cards | lands on | why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| md radius + shadow ("sheet") | 63 | `sheet` | — |
| md radius, no shadow ("plain") | 23 | `sheet` | the shadow is below the eye on manila (critique finding 5), so adding it changes nothing you can see |
| sm radius, no shadow ("ledger") | 21 | `ledger` | — |
| sm radius + shadow | 4 | `ledger` | the same reason: the radius is the part you can see, the shadow is not |

The issue's second comment asked whether "plain" (the Contract history ledger, the
player page grids) needs a third frame. On the evidence, no: the only thing
separating it from the sheet is a shadow nobody can see. `decisions.md` Q1 asks
Gary to confirm, and says what to do if he can see it.

### What `card.css` owns

- The frame: `border`, `border-radius`, `background`, `box-shadow`, and
  `overflow: hidden` so a band head's corners follow the card.
- `.card__body` padding for `body="padded"`.
- The interactive state for `as="a"` and `as="button"`: hover tint, `--focus-ring`,
  `cursor`, `text-align: inherit`, no underline. The tint mixes `var(--card-accent,
  var(--border-rule))`, so `.offdaycard` keeps its club tint by passing
  `--offday-accent` (ADR-0050 still gates that colour twice).

What it does NOT own, on purpose:

- **Margins.** 41 of the 111 rules set a margin. Spacing between cards is the
  parent's job, and #1180 (`Stack`/`Cluster`/`Grid`) is blocked on this issue's
  `body` axis. Until #1180 lands, each block keeps its margin in its namespace rule.
- **Layout inside the card** (grid, flex, gap, width). That stays in the namespace.
- **Accents on one block.** `.prospectcard`'s 3px top rule, `.levelprog`'s and
  `.ctr__tile`'s top rules stay namespace rules. One consumer is not a variant.
- **The ground.** Every card is `--surface-card`. 7 cards draw on another ground
  today. Six are box score sheets on `--paper-1` (which #1128 folded into the canvas
  colour): they change colour when they move — `decisions.md` Q3. The seventh,
  `.jerseydeck__card`, paints `var(--tint, var(--paper-1))`, a per-jersey tint; its
  namespace rule keeps that background and wins on order. One card
  (`.stars3__card`) is on inset paper and moves to card paper with the box score.

### The gap-rule grid (7 cards)

Seven cards draw their inner rules by painting the card `--border-rule` and letting
1px gaps between white cells show through (`.player__statgrid`,
`.player__splits`, `.factgrid`, `.umps__list`, `.bs__fill`, two /fouls tile rows).
`Card` paints `--surface-card`, so the grid moves to an inner element
(`<div className="x__grid">`) that keeps the `--border-rule` ground. This is a
markup change, not only CSS: screenshot each one.

### Four shadow-only sheets gain an edge

`.jerseydeck__card`, `.umpage__card`, `.umpage__list` and `.mgrpage__card` draw a
shadow and no border. On `Card` they gain the hairline edge. Small, visible, and in
the before/after screenshots.

### Nested frames — not fixed here

26 card-shaped rules are boxes inside a card: 16 panels (for example `.wcall`,
`.favormeter`, `.stat` inside the StatBox; `.lineup__list` inside `.lineup`;
`.thub-roster` inside `.thub-card__body`) and 10 inset wells. A card inside a card
is the "SaaS dashboard" drift the critique names. `Card` does not take them;
`decisions.md` Q4 asks whether to flatten them later.

### The spoiler scope

29 of the 111 cards have a consumer inside the spoiler scope: the innings viewer and
game HUD (slice C4), the box score's reveal render (C5), TeamInfo (C3) and the slate
(`.offdaycard`). The rule for those slices is **move the box, never the content**:

- Change the frame's class and delete the per-site frame rule. Do not move a
  `SealBox`, a `revealedThrough` check (the Pitchers table, ADR-0009) or a
  reveal-only call (ADR-0001).
- The box score cards render inside the `SealBox` reveal function
  (`screens/BoxScore.jsx`). They may take `<Card>` there, because `Card` imports
  nothing and computes nothing. `Card.jsx` and `SectionHead.jsx` must never import a
  stamp module or an `src/api/` module.
- 16 of those allowlist entries are band heads in these slices (the other three are
  the two lab mocks and `.umptend__bar`). That is why
  the band is its own slice (H1b).

### Cascade

`system/section-head.css` and `system/card.css` go into `src/index.css` right
**before** `06-loader-and-cards.css`. Every per-site partial from 06 on loads later,
so a namespace rule that keeps a margin or a grid wins on order at equal
specificity. Both must be in the core sheet (many screens share them), never a
per-route chunk. Pin the order with `test/card-cascade.test.js`, as
`test/pill-cascade.test.js` pins the pill.

### The lab

`/design-lab` gets a `Card` entry with a **composition** band: a sheet with a band
head and a flush list, a ledger with a label head and a padded body, an interactive
tile — at 390px and 740px (critique finding 11). The `.thub-card`, `.metriccard` and
other catalog entries in `screens/designlab/catalog.js` are renamed in the slice that
moves each block.
