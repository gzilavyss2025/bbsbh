# Offseason design study

Research and local wireframes only. No application files changed. No PR or deployment.

Open `index.html` through the local Vite server. The six PNG boards show three
concepts at desktop and mobile widths. All game, player and standings content
on the boards is illustrative. Read `research.md` for evidence, scope and decisions.

The drawings preserve the app's paper/ink palette, level navigation, club strip,
date navigation and roster-wire placement. They are annotated wireframes, not
screenshots of an implemented feature. Simplified club initials stand in for logos.

Validation and repository handoff are recorded in `handoff.md`.
