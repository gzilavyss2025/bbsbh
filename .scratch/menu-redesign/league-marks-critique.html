AL and NL marks on the club strip: critique

Your instinct is right. The league marks sit at the wrong end of the row, at the same size as the club logos, in a busier style. They read as a 16th club pinned to the far edge, not as labels for the rows.

Usability

FindingSeverityFix
The marks close the row on the right, after a wide gap. A label belongs where the eye starts a line, on the left. On the right, it's the last thing seen and is detached from the row it names.HighMove the league label to the start of each row.
At 40 px, the same as a club button, the crests look tappable, and tapping does nothing.MediumMake the label clearly not a button: text, or a much smaller, flatter mark.
"All" sits at the start of the AL row only, so it reads as part of the AL line.MediumGive All its own column, spanning both rows, to the left of the labels.

Visual hierarchy

  • What draws the eye first: the two full-color crests. Their banners, stars and fine detail are the busiest marks on the strip, and they shouldn't be.
  • Reading flow: All, then 15 clubs, then a gap, then a crest. The meaning arrives last.
  • Emphasis: the labels should be the quietest thing in the strip. They only sort; they don't pick.

Consistency

ElementIssueFix
League namingThe standings page just started saying "AL East" and "NL Central" in condensed caps labels. This strip uses crests instead.Use the same words in the same label style: "AL" and "NL", condensed caps, caption ink.
Mark styleThe crests come from MLB's full-color "on light" set. Every other league mark in the app (the standings bars) is the flat one-color version.If you keep a mark, use the flat one-color mark at about 20 px.

Accessibility

  • The marks are hidden from screen readers, which is right. The group would still read better with each row named "American League" or "National League".
  • Dark mode: the "on light" crests will look washed out on black. Text labels use ink tokens and need no dark-mode handling.

Recommended layout

[ALL]  AL  (A's)(BAL)(BOS)(CWS)(CLE)(DET)(HOU)(KC)(LAA)(MIN)(NYY)(SEA)(TB)(TEX)(TOR)
       NL  (AZ)(ATL)(CHC)(CIN)(COL)(LAD)(MIA)(MIL)(NYM)(PHI)(PIT)(SD)(SF)(STL)(WSH)
  • All on the left, centered across both rows.
  • "AL" and "NL" as small condensed labels at the start of each row, aligned to each other.
  • The clubs line up in two even columns of 15, so the rows read as a grid.

Priority fixes

  1. Replace the right-hand crests with "AL" and "NL" labels at the start of each row, in the standings' label style.
  2. Move All into its own column spanning both rows.
  3. Name each row for screen readers ("American League" / "National League").
