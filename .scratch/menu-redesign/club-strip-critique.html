Club strip on /leaders: critique

It now matches the home page and the pick works: tapping the Brewers highlights Jake Bauers and dims every other row. The biggest problem is the order: 30 logos in alphabetical order by city, split across a ragged second line, so finding a club means scanning every logo.

Usability

FindingSeverityFix
The clubs run alphabetically by city (Arizona, Athletics, Atlanta…) and wrap after 20, so line 2 holds 10 and the break means nothing.HighTwo rows by league: AL on one line, NL on the next, 15 each, in division order or by city. The break then carries meaning, and the rows come out even.

Visual hierarchy

  • What draws the eye first: the heavy stamped box. It's the loudest frame between the scope tabs and the leader cards, for what is a secondary control.
  • Reading flow: scope tabs → "Who is standing out" door → strip → Leaders. The door sits tight against the strip's top edge.
  • Emphasis: the picked club (ink edge, amber wash) stands out clearly from the 29 others.

Accessibility

  • Touch targets: club buttons are 40×36 px and All is 36×36 px, under the app's 44 px minimum (--tap-min), with 4 px between. Easy to miss on a phone.
  • Labels: buttons announce "MIL", "AZ". The old strip said "Milwaukee Brewers". A full name reads better to a screen reader.
  • Contrast: the "All" text is ink on a light amber wash, which passes easily. The logos are images and carry no text.
  • State: the pick uses aria-pressed, which is announced correctly.

What works well

  • Full-color logos are much faster to find than the old gray tray.
  • All 30 visible at once on desktop: no hidden sideways scroll.
  • The pressed club is unmistakable, and pressing it again goes back to All.

Priority fixes

  1. Two rows by league, AL then NL. Fixes the ragged break and makes a club findable by where it plays.
  2. 44 px buttons and full-name labels. Phone taps and screen readers.
  3. All keeps its outline when a club is picked, so the way back always looks like a button.
  4. Move ClubStrip to components/team/ with a framed option, then migrate the six old trays.
