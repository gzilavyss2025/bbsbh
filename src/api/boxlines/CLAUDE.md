# src/api/boxlines — Box Lines data and the Game lines registry (ADR-0069)

**`GameLinesCard` is a REGISTRY, not a card to copy** (ADR-0069, #997): a stack of `BoxLinesDoor`s, each a career line under one facet. Adding a facet (#998–#1006) is ONE entry — `{ key, label, note, title, facet, section, groups }` plus exactly ONE label source, `sitCode` (a situation), `careerGameType` (a career under a game type) or `fielding` (`'starts'`/`'bench'`, off the fielding career's own `gamesStarted`) — and nothing else: the fetch, the gate, the sheet and the dress are already there. The list itself lives in **`api/boxlines/cardFacets.js`**, not in this component: a `.jsx` file cannot be imported by `node --test`, and the failure that has to be pinned (an unknown facet `kind` keeps NOTHING, so a typo opens a door onto an empty ledger in silence) is otherwise untestable. Twenty-odd doors are lit — Home, Road, On grass, On turf, Day, Night, the eight months (#999), the seven weekdays (#1001), Postseason, a hitter's Pinch hitting (#1002) and his Started/Substitution (#1003), and for a pitcher only Started and In relief. They are drawn under FOUR HEADINGS (`SECTIONS` in the same registry file) and the card groups by that list, not by the registry's order, so a door added in the wrong place files itself instead of reordering the card; a heading with no door does not render. **The card is a TABLE** (ADR-0073): every door prints the same five figures, so they are named ONCE at each section's head (`DOOR_COLUMNS`) and each door is five cells under them (`doorCells`) — `label` stops being visible and becomes the sheet's headline and the button's accessible name, and the house `See all ›` is said once under the card title instead of on all twenty-five rows. Adding a facet needs nothing here; the columns are the group's, not the door's. Two rules the suite pins: the cells and `careerSplitLine` must quote the same five figures in the same order (the door is the only place the card and the sheet meet), and `DOOR_EMPHASIS` must ink a RATE — a pitcher's fifth column is `BB`, where a hitter's is OPS, and inking it there points at a walk total as though it were the headline. Emphasis is COLOUR, never weight: JetBrains Mono ships one registered weight here, so a "bold" cell renders identically to a plain one. A run of doors differing in one number — the eight months, the seven weekdays — sets `family` and FOLDS behind one row (`FAMILIES`/`FOLD_FROM`), so the card opens at ten lines rather than twenty-five; a folded door is not in the DOM, so a test asserting one opens its family first. The month and weekday doors cost nothing and, unlike Home/Road, their figures match the rows EXACTLY (a date is the one field the game log and the schedule cannot disagree about). They are also the only doors that **span the postseason** (`spansPostseason` + `facet.postseason`, ADR-0073): October means every October game, a Sunday in the World Series is a Sunday. Both fields are required together and pinned by test — one alone states a career the door does not open. MLB publishes no combined aggregate (`careerStatSplits` answers one `gameType` at a time and `R,P` returns the rows separately, twice over), so the label adds two with `mergeCareerSplits`, recomputing rates from components — two rates cannot be averaged, and MLB's OPS is each half ROUNDED to three places then added. No November door exists: sitCode `11` returns nothing. Pinch hitting reads the hitting game log's own `positionsPlayed` — `['PH', 'LF']` entered as a pinch hitter and stayed in — so it needs no boxscore, no cap and no paging, whatever #1002 specifies. The Postseason door (#1006) is the one that is NOT free: its rows are not the regular season, so it moves the fetch's game types instead of filtering rows, and it does not share the other six's join. Its rows wear a round pill (WC/DS/LCS/WS); never ask statsapi for the umbrella `P`, which comes back as every PITCHING row's type and empties the sheet for pitchers alone (ADR-0069). **On grass / On turf** ride on `hydrate=venue(fieldInfo)`, +7% on the shared schedule call, and the surface is SEASON-correct (Chase Field is grass through 2018, turf from 2019) — never build this off a table of today's parks. A hitter's **Started / Substitution** (#1003) are the two doors whose figures are a game COUNT and nothing else, because their source is a fielding career with no rate stat in it: they set `lineKind: 'games'` alongside `fielding`, and `doorLine` is what lets one card hold both kinds of line. They are also the only doors that cost a request of their own — `needsLineups` sends `fetch.js` back for the schedule's lineups, because folding those into the shared call costs +65% where two doors of twenty-five want them. A row the lineups could not answer for is `null` and belongs to NEITHER door. A door's figure is MLB's career aggregate and its rows are MLB's per-game flags; those two disagree by a few relocated games and cannot be reconciled, so never assert one against the other. **A LIST DOOR IS THE OTHER SHAPE** (#1048): an entry with a `list` descriptor and NO `facet` and NO label source, for a question with too many answers to be doors. `{ groupBy, name, order, facet, title }` — the sheet folds the gated rows into groups and each group opens its own rows (`api/boxlines/fold.js`). Its figures come from the ROWS and not from MLB, and that is forced: `b1`–`b9` count games with a plate appearance in a slot where the lineups count who STARTED there, so a door labelled from the aggregate opens an empty sheet on the low slots. It names no source, so the card cannot test it for games and it renders whenever the card does — but the card's existence test asks for at least one SOURCED door, or a MiLB player would get a card holding one. Its row is its name and the chevron, no five cells: the figures belong to its groups. Two are lit: `By spot in the order` (hitters only, #1048) and `By ballpark` (both groups, #998) — the second cost a descriptor and a name, which is the evidence the shape is general. A park list groups on the stable venue ID and names from the group's NEWEST row, because nine of a career's 36 parks carry more than one name inside it. **Both lists count October** (`postseason: true` on the list's facet) — a park does not stop being Dodger Stadium because the game was a division series, and Betts at Globe Life Field is 9 games without it and 25 with. A list door must never set `spansPostseason`: that widens a LABEL's fetch and a list has no label source, so a test pins it undefined. Do not build a second facet card beside it
