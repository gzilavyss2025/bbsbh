# src/components/worldseries — the World Series home (#1906)

`WorldSeriesHome` is mounted by `GameSelect` on every postseason-window date and draws
nothing unless `lib/postseason/worldseries/mode.js` says the Series is on (both clubs
known, not decided heading into the slate date). It replaces the bracket fold and the
series primer then. Hero on a phone; hero plus `Ledger` table from 740px.

- **Spoiler rule (ADR-0087).** Past games show their runs; today's game shows no score
  slot at all (no seal, no button: ADR-0116). The gate is
  `lib/postseason/worldseries/ledger.js`, never a component: pass it everything, read
  only its cells. Every read ends the day before the
  slate date (`api/postseason/worldSeriesHome.js`).
- **Parts, not copies.** Cards are stamped `Card`, figures `StatStrip`/`Table`, legends
  `Key`, controls `Button`/`Door`. New shapes (schedule strip, fence overlay, pitch bars)
  live here under the `ww-` namespace in `styles/worldseries/`. No new token: the mockup's
  two fills are `--surface-stamped-tint` and `--accent-tint`.
- `Hero` ("Overview", Championship Matchup approved 2026-10-21; `styles/worldseries/hero.css`) centres the next game's date between player layers, with mono club marks, win pips, the Series mark and "Best of seven". Postseason spotlight names, images, figures and labeled highlights follow the spotlight player's identity (`slabPlayers`' first entry): chosen IDs before Game 1 (`PRE_SERIES_PICKS` in `lib/postseason/worldseries/hero.js`), then best OPS with 25+ PA. Names are live split data, never artwork. `calendarDays` and `withFormatDates` retain the 2-3-2 calendar; `ScheduleStrip` wraps to five columns on phones and shows Today/Next. Conditional games alone have dashed edges. A tap on the art (Gary, 2026-10-23) slides both slabs out and the next pair in, walking each club's whole roster: `spotlightRoster` orders it (the pre-Series pick, postseason hitters by OPS, postseason pitchers by innings, then everyone with no October line by regular-season PA or innings) and gives each player a stat line (his postseason line, else his regular-season one marked "Season", else "No stats yet"); until `api/postseason/worldseries/spotlightPool.js` has loaded the roster the slabs cycle the hot-bat hitters, who lead the same list. Highlights are stored, not fetched: `storedClip` reads the clubs' `public/data/highlights/{teamId}.json` (nightly `gen-highlights.mjs`), the newest play clip crediting the player from a game dated before the slate date, a Series postseason game first and else his newest this season (a pitcher's is a pitching clip, a hitter's is not). On 2026-10-23, 51 of the 52 players on both rosters had one in those files (the Rays' Garrett Cleavinger had none, so he shows no button; the files start 2026-07-28 and `gen-highlights-backfill.mjs` can reach earlier). No `/content` read waits on the page. The hero's ledger columns have no runs; today's game has no score. The hero ends on the slate's own `GameCard` for the next game (Gary, 2026-10-23), in place of its lineups bar: `GameSelect` hands `WorldSeriesHome` `seriesGame` (today's, on a game day) and `renderCard`; on an off day the home fetches the next game's row from its own date (`fetchSchedule`) and the card opens that date. A page with no slate (the live series page) passes neither and keeps the one preview/lineups door, which uses `nextGamePath` (the game's own date). Existing slate split/scale (`--versus`) and session arrival motion settle in about a second; the card is usable throughout and reduced motion draws the resting state.
- `Reel` ("Series reel", or "Road here" before Game 1 is played) is a snap rail with a Chips filter; clip and photo logic is `lib/postseason/worldseries/reel.js`.
- `cards/` holds Probables, Tale of the tape and Hot bats: `leadSide` marks the tile that leads the two clubs with `.ww-best` (`--surface-stamped-tint`).
- `cards/scouting/Platoon.jsx` + `ParkFit.jsx` (jump-bar items "Platoon" and "Park fit", no count; `fit.css`). Platoon: each club's regular-season lineup OPS vs right- and left-handers (team `statSplits`, `api/scout/seriesFit.js`); the higher rounded OPS wears `.ww-best` (a tie leads for nobody) and the cell for the hand a club faces in the focus game (the other club's probable, `scouting/platoon.js` `facedHands`) is tinted. Park fit: both clubs' home parks over each other (`FenceOverlay.jsx`; the pair comes from `fitParks`: the static teams file's `venueId`, or the focus game's own venue for the club that hosts it), above the pull, centre and opposite-field share of the clubs' regulars' fly balls (launch angle 25 to 50, `spray` store, `scouting/parkFit.js`). One infield (`components/ballpark/BallparkInfield.jsx`), then each park's ground, foul lines, wall and signs; a park's ink follows its club (`ink`), never its place in `sides`. A `Segmented` control picks Both or one park (`parkView` in `scouting/parkFit.js` derives which, and is tested): the other wall draws back, the header names the park, and the table gains "Carry past wall", the share of each club's fly balls per direction that travel farther than that wall on their own angle (`carryRates`). That block is an ESTIMATE and the card says so: a ball counts when its distance is more than the wall's distance plus the wall's height on its angle (`wallHeightAt`; a 45 degree descent is an assumption), and a direction under `MIN_CARRY` (20, Gary 2026-10-21) balls is a dash. The card opens on Both on every day (Gary 2026-10-21). Signs that would overlap step inside the nearer wall (`scouting/fenceOverlay.js`). Motion is in `fit.css`, all under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. It shows on both day types, with one park when only one has a layout. Both mount through `ScoutingReport`, which takes the focus `game` row.
- `cards/` also holds Bullpen, The trip, Home and road and Played. Stamp is a door to the Game Log flow (`logbookNewPath`), so no stamp part is imported.
- `ScoutingReport` (#2316, ADR-0034 open surface) is the navy band, a sticky jump bar (`HubTabBar variant="anchor" badges={false}`) and the scouting cards. Mounted once in `WorldSeriesHome`: after `Reel` on an off day, after `Probables` on a game day. The card list, its order and the day-type rule are `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/scouting.js` (`scoutingModel`): off day = Nine keys, Glove and legs, Roster, Matchup edges, Pitch by pitch, Platoon, Park fit, Film room; game day = Matchup edges, Umpire edge (once a plate umpire is posted), Pitch by pitch, Platoon, Film room, Park fit, Glove and legs, Roster. The model files live in `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/`. The card order and its rules are also in `docs/world-series-scouting.md`. A card with no data drops, and its button with it. Matchup edges has three states (`scouting/edgesState.js`): edges, `none` (the career-matchups file has a row for the game but no hitter clears a threshold: the card stays with one line, "No standout matchups for this game."), and `absent` (no row, or the file failed: the card drops). A card whose load FAILED drops too, and one plain line under the jump bar names it (`scouting/failedCards.js`; the loaders throw, never swallow). `WorldSeriesHome` passes its one `focusPk` (the next game on an off day, today's on a game day), so the edges and the umpire read the same game. The jump bar sticks under the slate's own bar: `--slate-bar-h` (`styles/slate/masthead.css`) in CSS, and a probe in JS, feed one measured `--ww-scout-offset`. A new card is one entry in `scouting.js` plus one renderer in `ScoutingReport.jsx`. It keys on `clubIds`, never on a club.
- `cards/scouting/FilmRoom.jsx` + `FilmMeeting.jsx` (jump-bar item "Film", no count; a card is the box score's own look: a line score, decisions, a Pitching table (surname first, seven columns), each starter's pitch mix, the scoring plays under the club's mark and ▲/▼ + inning, each with a ▶ that opens the pitch's raw clip (`useWatchClip`, `HighlightSheet`; `playId` is the plate appearance's last pitch, resolved only on the tap; these games are past and their scores are open on the card, so the clip's burned-in scorebug leaks nothing), a Lineups toggle, then the Box score door; one column, two across from 740px) draw the clubs' past regular-season meetings from `api/postseason/meetings.js` `fetchMeetings({ clubA, clubB, slateDate })` (not the older `worldSeriesHome.js` one). Off day: full cards; game day: compact (line score, decisions, Box score door). The Box score door is a plain link; it sets no flag and writes no `bbsbh:boxreveal:*` key, and this folder imports no reveal-only module (ADR-0117 addendum).
- `cards/scouting/ComparisonLedger.jsx` draws the home-only `NineKeys.jsx` and `GloveLegs.jsx` in Design A (2026-10-21): matching internal titles, regular-season labels, and measure → first club → second club columns (`sides` order). It is a club adapter over the shared `ui/table/PairLedger.jsx` (#2455); leaders have an amber underline and hidden "leads". Nine keys reads `data.bar` for clay failed-rank asterisks; the series pages keep `SeriesNineKeys`. Glove and legs keeps its six measures and Defense/Running groups; `scouting/gloveLegs.js` still drops it when fewer than 4 measures are real for both clubs. A null figure draws "—" with hidden "no figure"; a tie marks nobody. Styles: `styles/system/ledger/pair-ledger.css`, plus `comparison.css` and `glove.css` for a few leftovers; contracts: `docs/world-series-scouting.md`, `docs/design-system-pair-ledger.md`.
- `cards/scouting/PitchByPitch.jsx` (jump-bar item "Pitch by pitch"; off day rank 4, game day rank 3) draws the favorite Series club's starter (the named probable, else the projected one) against the other club's nine hitters with the most postseason PA, not the posted lineup. Reads: `api/scout/pitchByPitch.js`; the table rows and the Arm/Bats/Even call (`EDGE_BAND` .020, chosen by Gary 2026-10-20; `MIN_PA`, `MIN_USE`, `MAX_HITTERS` are still placeholders) are `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/pitchByPitch.js`. Open surface; every read ends the day before the slate date.
- `cards/scouting/Roster.jsx` (jump-bar item "Roster", full width; `roster.css`) draws each Series club's roster by position group (`scouting/roster.js`), club tabs and one stat-set toggle as `Segmented`, rows as ghost block Buttons that open the player's line inline; reads `api/scout/rosterStats.js` (every read ends the day before the slate date), rules in `docs/world-series-scouting.md`.
- **The live series page mounts the same pieces** (`/postseason/{id}`, World Series ids only; `screens/postseason-live/LiveSeriesPage.jsx`, layout C from the 2026-10-22 wireframe). `seriesMode(series)` in `mode.js` builds the mode shape from one series object (no `decided` gate; `worldSeriesMode` calls it), and `hooks/postseason/useWorldSeriesView.js` is the data both `WorldSeriesHome` and the page read (home data, focus game, plate umpire, played rows). The page draws `Hero` (an undecided Series; a decided one keeps its banner), then `GroupJump` (the one sticky bar) over `Group` sections: Now (Today, Starting pitchers, Still to play), Series (Game by game and its neighbours, `Reel`), Scout (`ScoutingReport`), Compare (Tale of the tape, Hot bats, The trip, Home and road, Bullpen, the Regular season strip, `FilmRoom`, Nine Keys, ABS report, Former teammates), Rosters (`RosterCard`). `lib/postseason/worldseries/groups.js` is the list; a group with no data drops with its button. The page passes `ScoutingReport` `jumpBar={false}` (one bar, not two), `omit={['nine-keys', 'film-room']}` (the page has `SeriesNineKeys`, and Film room sits in Compare) and `series={null}` (no Roster card: `RosterCard` stays), and `onShown` tells the page whether the Scout group has any card. Every read still ends the day before the page's cutoff, passed as the slate date. **The finished page uses the same parts** (`PostseasonSeriesPage.jsx`, World Series ids only; other rounds keep their old order): `historySeriesMode(h)` in `mode.js` builds the mode shape from a `postseason-history.json` series (no games ahead) and the cutoff (the day after the last game, so every read ends on the Series' last day), `useWorldSeriesView` and `GroupJump`/`Group` are shared, and `screens/postseason-live/FinishedGroups.jsx` mounts Series, Scout, Compare and Rosters. The banner with the trophy and the Series MVP card stay on top: `Hero` draws nothing for a decided Series. No Now group, Trip, Today or Starting pitchers. `.psseries--ws` carries the live page's jump-bar CSS (`series-live.css`).
