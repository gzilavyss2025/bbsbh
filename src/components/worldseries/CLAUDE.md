# src/components/worldseries — the World Series home (#1906)

`WorldSeriesHome` is mounted by `GameSelect` on every postseason-window date and draws
nothing unless `lib/postseason/worldseries/mode.js` says the Series is on (both clubs
known, not decided heading into the slate date). It replaces the bracket fold and the
series primer then. Hero on a phone; hero plus `Ledger` table from 740px.

- **Spoiler rule (ADR-0087).** Past games show their runs; today's game shows no score
  slot at all (no seal, no button: ADR-0116). The gate is
  `lib/postseason/worldseries/ledger.js`, never a component: pass it everything, read
  only its cells. Every read ends the day before the
  slate date (`api/postseason/worldSeriesHome.js`).
- **Parts, not copies.** Cards are stamped `Card`, figures `StatStrip`/`Table`, legends
  `Key`, controls `Button`/`Door`. New shapes (schedule strip, fence overlay, pitch bars)
  live here under the `ww-` namespace in `styles/worldseries/`. No new token: the mockup's
  two fills are `--surface-stamped-tint` and `--accent-tint`.
- `Reel` ("Series reel", or "Road here" before Game 1 is played) is a snap rail with a Chips filter; clip and photo logic is `lib/postseason/worldseries/reel.js`.
- `cards/` holds Probables, Tale of the tape and Hot bats: `leadSide` marks the tile that leads the two clubs with `.ww-best` (`--surface-stamped-tint`).
- `cards/scouting/Platoon.jsx` + `ParkFit.jsx` (jump-bar items "Platoon" and "Park fit", no count; `fit.css`). Platoon: each club's regular-season lineup OPS vs right- and left-handers (team `statSplits`, `api/scout/seriesFit.js`); the higher rounded OPS wears `.ww-best` (a tie leads for nobody) and the cell for the hand a club faces in the focus game (the other club's probable, `scouting/platoon.js` `facedHands`) is tinted. Park fit: `BallparkDiagram` for Milwaukee over the pull, centre and opposite-field share of the clubs' regulars' fly balls (launch angle 25 to 50, `spray` store, `scouting/parkFit.js`). It always draws Milwaukee: the other club's park is not on file for Games 3 to 5. Both mount through `ScoutingReport`, which takes the focus `game` row.
- `cards/` also holds Bullpen, The trip, Home and road and Played. Stamp is a door to the Game Log flow (`logbookNewPath`), so no stamp part is imported.
- `ScoutingReport` (#2316, ADR-0034 open surface) is the navy band, a sticky jump bar (`HubTabBar variant="anchor" badges={false}`) and the scouting cards. Mounted once in `WorldSeriesHome`: after `Reel` on an off day, after `Probables` on a game day. The card list, its order and the day-type rule are `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/scouting.js` (`scoutingModel`): off day = Nine keys, Glove and legs, Matchup edges, Pitch by pitch, Platoon, Park fit, Film room; game day = Matchup edges, Umpire edge (once a plate umpire is posted), Pitch by pitch, Platoon, Film room, Park fit (only when tonight's park is Milwaukee, venue 32), Glove and legs. The model files live in `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/`. The card order and its rules are also in `docs/world-series-scouting.md`. A card with no data drops, and its button with it. Matchup edges has three states (`scouting/edgesState.js`): edges, `none` (the career-matchups file has a row for the game but no hitter clears a threshold: the card stays with one line, "No standout matchups for this game."), and `absent` (no row, or the file failed: the card drops). A card whose load FAILED drops too, and one plain line under the jump bar names it (`scouting/failedCards.js`; the loaders throw, never swallow). `WorldSeriesHome` passes its one `focusPk` (the next game on an off day, today's on a game day), so the edges and the umpire read the same game. The jump bar sticks under the slate's own bar: `--slate-bar-h` (`styles/slate/masthead.css`) in CSS, and a probe in JS, feed one measured `--ww-scout-offset`. A new card is one entry in `scouting.js` plus one renderer in `ScoutingReport.jsx`. It keys on `clubIds`, never on a club.
- `cards/scouting/FilmRoom.jsx` + `FilmMeeting.jsx` (jump-bar item "Film", no count) draw the clubs' past regular-season meetings from `api/postseason/meetings.js` `fetchMeetings({ clubA, clubB, slateDate })` (not the older `worldSeriesHome.js` one). Off day: full cards on a snap rail (three across from 740px); game day: compact. The Box score door is a plain link; it sets no flag and writes no `bbsbh:boxreveal:*` key, and this folder imports no reveal-only module (ADR-0117 addendum).
- `cards/scouting/GloveLegs.jsx` (jump-bar item "Glove and legs", no count; both day types, last on a game day) compares the clubs' defense and running on the Tale of the tape rows. `scouting/gloveLegs.js` (`gloveLegsModel`) holds the six measures, the leader of each (`.ww-best`, `--marker`) and the rule that drops the card: fewer than 4 of 6 measures real for both clubs. The `Table` has a `<thead>` of club heads (logo and name, in `sides` order, with a hidden "Measure" head between), and the card wears the `Card` name tab with a "Regular season" count. Each figure is a `Pill mark` in it (leader `fill="marker"`, hidden "leads"); a null figure draws "—" with hidden "no figure".
- `cards/scouting/PitchByPitch.jsx` (jump-bar item "Pitch by pitch"; off day rank 4, game day rank 3) draws the favorite Series club's starter (the named probable, else the projected one) against the other club's nine hitters with the most postseason PA, not the posted lineup. Reads: `api/scout/pitchByPitch.js`; the table rows and the Arm/Bats/Even call (`EDGE_BAND` .020, chosen by Gary 2026-10-20; `MIN_PA`, `MIN_USE`, `MAX_HITTERS` are still placeholders) are `lib/postseason/worldseries/scouting/pitchByPitch.js`. Open surface; every read ends the day before the slate date.
