Tally Baseball / design study

A field behind the names

Four treatments at the size of the lineup and box score defense card. Each uses the same defenders. Compare the field shape, contrast, and how easily you can find a position.

Starting defenders from MIL at STL, July 7, 2026, game 2 (gamePk 823035).

How these would ship: One small inline SVG in the existing DefenseDiamond component. It adds no image request, raster asset, font, or dependency. Names stay HTML above the drawing, so links and substitution stacks keep their present behavior.

Option B follows the straight grass edges, curved dirt boundary, and circular cutouts in the MLB field diagram and this Chase Field photo. Its outfield is shortened to fit the card. Base markers are enlarged for legibility.
