The wire on Rule 5 protection day

What the home page's transactions wire says when clubs add players to the 40-man to protect them from the Rule 5 draft, and how to fix it. Brewers, Nov. 17, 2026. 2026-11-23.

Short answer: yes, and it is partly a bug fix. The wire tells a November 40-man add as a call-up to the majors, in the present tense, mid-season. Every piece needed to tell it right (the date, the player's signing and birth date, his prospect rank) is already in hand.

What it says today

Selected the contracts of C Matthew Wood and 2B Dylan O'Rae from Biloxi Shuckers. First call-up. The 25-year-old is hitting .257 with 18 homers in 95 games in Double-A. Wood was the Brewers' fourth-round pick… He gets his first call to the majors.
ProblemWhy it's wrong
"First call-up" / "his first call to the majors"A contract selected in November is a 40-man add. Nobody is called up; there are no games. The template treats every selection by a player without a debut as a call-up (templates.js, `kind === 'selected'`).
"is hitting .257"The season is over. The line is his finished 2026, so "hit".
The why is missingThe one fact a reader wants (he was added so another club can't take him in the Rule 5 draft) is never said.
Five separate storiesProtection day is one event per club. The Brewers' adds arrive as two stories (one per affiliate) of near-identical sentences.

What we already know

  • Offseason: a selection between the end of the season and spring training is a 40-man add, never a call-up.
  • Rule 5 eligibility: rule5Year(person) (lib/trade/pedigree.js) already computes the December a player first becomes eligible, from his birth date and first contract (draft or international signing). The wire's player read (playerFacts.js) already fetches both.
  • Prospect rank: already in the facts (MLB top 100, club top 30).

Recommended changes, in order

  1. Offseason wording (the bug). In the offseason a selection reads as an add, in the past tense:
    Added to the 40-man roster, protecting him from the Rule 5 draft. Wood, 25, hit .257 with 18 homers in 95 games in Double-A in 2026.
    "protecting him from the Rule 5 draft" only when rule5Year says he is eligible this December; otherwise just "Added to the 40-man roster."
  2. A kicker on the row: "Rule 5 protection" as the story's type label, the way trades read "Trade". Scannable down the column on deadline day.
  3. One story per club on deadline day: the club's adds folded into one row, its players' notes beneath, as the wire already folds a busy day:
    Added five to the 40-man roster ahead of the Rule 5 draft: C Matthew Wood, 2B Dylan O'Rae, SS Jett Williams (No. 10 prospect), 3B Luke Adams, LHP Tate Kuehner.
  4. Later, worth a look: who a club left exposed: ranked prospects eligible this December who were not added. Derivable from the prospect lists and rule5Year, and it is the story fans read about the day after. A bigger piece, a separate decision.

Caveats

  • The protection deadline moves each year (mid-to-late November). Items 1–2 key on "offseason + eligible this December", not on the date, so they need no calendar.
  • A player added who is not Rule 5 eligible yet (rare, usually a near-ready prospect) just reads "Added to the 40-man roster."
  • The cutline itself ("Selected the contracts of… from Biloxi Shuckers") comes from MLB's wire and stays as filed.
