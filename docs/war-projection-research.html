How others project future WAR

Research for the trade simulator's Proj. WAR column (src/lib/trade/value.js). 2026-11-23.

Bottom line: our age penalty (−0.5 WAR a year from 30) is the industry's standard rule of thumb. What makes Bauers look low is the first step: a flat pull toward 1 WAR that doesn't care how much he played. Fix that first; the age step is close to right, but should get steeper after 35.

All three share our 1.7 starting point, which is where most of the gap to his 3.6 season comes from.

Recommendation

  1. Scale the pull toward average by playing time. Weight each season by its plate appearances or innings, as Marcel does, so a full season counts for more than a cup of coffee. This is the change that matters for Bauers.
  2. Keep −0.5 for ages 30–34; make it −0.75 from 35. That matches the published curves and is barely any code.
  3. Leave $8.5M per WAR. It sits inside the range of current estimates.
