Dashboard review: Trade Simulator

Seen rendered at /trade-simulator with a live deal (Aaron Ashby to Boston for Adley Rutschman), at 1440px, 868px, 768px and 375px. No horizontal page scroll at any of those widths. Dark mode and keyboard-only were not checked.

Must fix (misleading numbers)

WhereWhy it mattersFix
Roster rows, salary column: Jeferson Quero $75K, Colton Gordon $97K, Garrett Stallings $123K, Bo Naylor $185K.These are below the $780K league minimum. They look like pay earned in the majors this year (prorated), but they read as salary. The value model itself uses $780K, so the row and the scoreboard disagree.Show the contract salary, with the league minimum as the floor for a pre-arbitration player. Keep earned pay off the row.
Season labels: the rows show "WAR" and a salary with no year. The deal sheet shows "2026 WAR", "Better in 2027" and "2027 payroll". Rutschman's row says $2.1M, but his club's 2027 payroll moves by $5.2M.Three seasons share one screen, and only some numbers say which one. A reader can't line the figures up.Put the year on every number: "2026 WAR" in the row's column head and "2026 salary" (or 2027, matching the sheet). Use one season for money everywhere.

Should fix (slow to read)

WhereWhy it mattersFix
First load, before a partner is picked: an empty yellow scoreboard bar and an empty card under it (desktop).The page's first view is blank boxes, with no next step.Hide the scoreboard until there is a partner. Put one line in its place: "Pick a trade partner to start."
Trade-partner logo rail: cut off at the right edge, at desktop and phone width.Nothing shows that it scrolls, so 20 or more clubs look missing.Let it wrap into rows at the wide step. On a phone, fade the right edge or show half a logo.
Scoreboard: "B" next to each club, with "Fair trade" in the middle.The letter has no label, so it isn't clear what is being graded.Add a small caption under each grade, such as "deal grade".
Rows with no data (Cameron Wagoner, Tate Kuehner, Matthew Wood): "— — WAR".Three dashes and a label say nothing.One phrase in their place: "No big-league time". The prospect stamp already does this job for ranked prospects.

Nice to have

WhereFix
Sort menu: 8 options, including "OPS / ERA vs level".Rename it to "Vs. his level", and drop options most readers won't use.
"How it's figured": the page text reads "How it's figured I". The icon's letter is leaking into the accessible name.Mark the icon aria-hidden.

Decisions (Gary, 2026-11-19)

  1. Salary: show the contract salary, never less than the $780K minimum.
  2. Season: salary is for 2027. WAR is 2026 until the 2027 season starts, then 2027. Label the year on every figure.
  3. First view: hide the scoreboard until a partner is picked, and show "Pick a trade partner to start."
  4. Logo strip: wrap all 29 clubs into rows.
  5. Grade: caption "Deal grade" under each letter.
  6. No-data rows: show his minors line in the stat slot, for example "AA · 3.12 ERA".
  7. Polish: rename the sort option to "Vs. his level", and hide the icon from screen readers.

What works

  • The tug-of-war meter and the club-coloured header give the verdict in one glance.
  • A traded player stays in his roster row, greyed out, with "Being traded to Red Sox". The deal's state is clear without leaving the list.
  • The deal sheet uses three lines (wins, long-term value, payroll), mirrored on each side. That is the right small number of figures.
