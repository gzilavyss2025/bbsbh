Trade Simulator: one club band per side

Today each club shows up three times in a row: in the scoreboard, on the deal card's bar and on the roster card's bar. Both sketches name each club once.

A. The scoreboard is the header for each column

The scoreboard's two coloured halves become the tops of the two columns. Under each half, one card holds what that club gets, then its roster. The deal grade and trade meter stay in the middle. There are no club bars lower down.

B. Rosters on top, the deal in a tray at the bottom

Each roster card shows its club as a coloured left edge and a small title, with no full-width bar. The deal (what each club gets, the grades and the meter) sits in a tray that stays at the bottom of the screen while you pick players. On a phone the tray stacks: the meter on top, the two clubs below.
