Trade roster card: control hierarchy

The head of each roster card on /trade-simulator (club bar, what to add, which list, position, search, sort). Reviewed on Twins vs Tigers at desktop width and at 375 px. 2026-11-23.

Verdict: the order is right (what to add → which list → position → sort), but every level wears the same chip, so nothing says which choice is the big one. On a phone the controls take half the screen before the first player, and the sort row breaks.

What the card asks, in order

Club bar        Minnesota Twins                      ✕ (change club)
1 What to add   [Players] → folds open: Players · Picks · Cash
2 Which list    Everyone · 40-man · Prospects        🔍
3 Position      All · Pitchers · Catchers · Infield · Outfield
4 Sort          Player  [Sort by…]   Salary  Yrs   WAR ▾

Must fix

  1. The sort row breaks on a phone. Roster list head, 375 px. "WAR ▾" drops to a second line under "Player", and the Sort by box crowds Salary. Made worse by the outlined Sort by box added today. Fix: on a phone, move Sort by out of the column heads into the position row's end (or under it), and keep the heads to one line over their figures.
  2. Half a phone screen of controls before the first player. Rows 1–4, 375 px: about 270 px of a 406 px card. Outfield wraps onto its own line. Fix: make the position row one line that scrolls sideways, and fold "What to add" into the same line as "Which list" (it already folds to one chip).

Should fix

  1. The top-level choice looks like any other chip. "Players" chip, row 1. It is the biggest decision on the card (add players, picks or cash), but it looks like a selected filter, and nothing says that tapping it shows Picks and Cash. Fix: draw Players · Picks · Cash as tabs across the top of the card (the underline tab the player page uses), always visible; the list and position chips stay below as filters.
  2. Two filter rows, same look, no grouping. Rows 2 and 3. "Which list" and "Position" are separate questions but are identical bordered chip strips. Fix: once row 1 is tabs, keep rows 2 and 3 as chips; a little more space between row 2 and row 3 than inside each makes them read as two groups.
  3. The search glass floats. Right edge, between rows 1 and 2 on a phone. It belongs to the list, but it is not lined up with anything. Fix: put it at the end of the "Which list" row, on the same line, at every width.
  4. The ✕ in the club bar reads as "close". Club bar, top right. It actually changes the club. Fix: the club name itself as the door ("Minnesota Twins ▾"), or a "Change" text button, instead of an ✕.

Nice to have

  1. Column heads don't look tappable. Player, Salary, Yrs, WAR. Only the active one shows ▾. A faint ▾ on hover/focus would hint the rest sort too.
  2. "Everyone" is vague. Row 2. It means the whole organization. "Whole org" or "All levels" says what it adds over 40-man and Prospects.
