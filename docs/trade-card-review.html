Trade card review

A UI/UX review of docs/trade-card.html, checked against the ui-ux-pro-max priority list (accessibility, touch, layout, type and colour, forms, navigation). Phone checked at 375 px, desktop at 800 px. 2026-11-18.

Verdict: the layout and the carbon-form look hold up. The real problems are at phone width: the player table is cut off, and the tap targets are too small. Fix those two before this becomes the app page.

SeverityAreaFindingFix
HighLayoutAt 375 px the six-column player table runs past the edge. The WAR column and the × remove button are cut off, and two-word names wrap onto two lines.On phones, show each player as a two-line row: name and tags on line 1, then age · pay · control · WAR on line 2. Put the remove button on the right.
HighTouchThe × remove button is about 20 px, and the roster chips are about 28 px tall. Both are under the 44 px minimum, and the × sits right against the WAR number.Make both at least 44 px tall. Give each chip at least 8 px of space.
HighContrastThe grey text (40-man line, positions, "What moves" labels) is about 3.9:1 on the paper, under the 4.5:1 that small text needs.Darken the grey to about #5d636b in light mode.
MediumClarityThe stamp is the only way to switch between Reported and Official, but it looks like decoration. Nothing says you can tap it.In the app it should flip on its own when the trade reaches the wire. For a manual "I heard about this" case, add a labelled toggle near it.
MediumFlowOnce a side has a player, its roster folds shut. Adding a second player takes an extra tap, and the main action is hidden.Keep the roster open on desktop. On phones, use a sticky "Add player" button that opens a sheet with search.
MediumDeep link"Copy link" builds ?a=&b=&p=, but the page never reads those values when it loads. A shared link opens the default trade.Read the query string when the page loads. In the app, this is the route's search params.
MediumAccessibilityEach club picker has both a <label for> and an aria-label ("Club one"), so a screen reader hears "Club one" instead of "Brewers send". The totals do not announce when they change.Drop the aria-label. Add aria-live="polite" to the notes list.
MediumDataA real 40-man roster has 40+ names. A wall of chips will not scale.Group by position (SP, RP, C, IF, OF, prospects) and add a search box.
LowColourNegative numbers are red, but each also has a minus sign, so meaning does not rely on colour alone. Red also marks a no-trade clause, which reads as "bad" when it is only a fact.Keep red for the stamp and for flags that block the deal. Show minus amounts in ink.
LowCopy"Form T-2 · two-club deal" is decoration that looks like data.Cut it, or use it for the trade date.
LowDesign systemThe page uses its own fonts and colours, not the app's tokens (src/styles/). Special Elite and Archivo Narrow are not in the app.When it's built in the app, map it to the paper-scorebook tokens and keep only the stamp as the page's signature.
LowMotionThe stamp turn is the only animation, and it respects reduced motion. Good.None.

What works: the empty-state line tells you what to do. The notes are plain sentences, not a score. Swap sides and the disabled duplicate-club option prevent an impossible trade. Focus rings are visible.
