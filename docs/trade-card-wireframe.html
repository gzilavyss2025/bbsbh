Trade Card

Wireframe for the trade page. Pick two clubs, tap players off each roster, and the card shows what each side gives up. There is no "who wins" grade. Placeholder players and numbers: layout only.

What to react to

  • Reported / Official switch. Reported is a deal you heard about. It flips to Official and links the transaction once the trade reaches the wire.
  • Control counts seasons the new club keeps him (contract years or arbitration). In baseball this matters more than age.
  • Flags are facts, not opinions: a 40-man roster crunch, a no-trade clause, 10-and-5 rights, the luxury-tax line.
  • No grade on purpose. Every value tool gets the same complaint: "it approved an absurd trade."
  • Phone: the two sides stack. Desktop: they sit side by side, with totals across the bottom.
