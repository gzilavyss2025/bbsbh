Trade card: two reviewers

Two critical ease-of-use reviews of /trade, each run by a separate agent on the live dev server, 2026-11-19.

  • Dana: a dynasty fantasy manager, phone on the couch, wants answers in two taps.
  • Marcus: a retired pro scout with front-office years, tablet, distrusts any number he can't vouch for.

Both said the browser's emulated width kept jumping, so a few layout notes are tentative. Neither reached every view.

Where they agree:
  • One tap can silently wipe half the deal.
  • The depth grid's before-and-after counts read as one number ("32", "2122").
  • The deal summary sits too far down.
Where they differ: Dana wants fewer controls (hide the retain options). Marcus wants more precision: real arbitration estimates, option status, and the season and source for WAR.

What both liked

  • "How he got here" ("Trade · CWS '25 · R1 #3 '19"), with See the trade one tap away.
  • What moves, mirrored, with no "who wins" grade.
  • The roster sheet: search, position filters, the prospect-rank and level sorts, prospect ranks on the rows, and the 40-man count in each card's header. Dana: "better than KTC for whether a deal is realistic."

Notes on their findings

  • The "32" / "2122" read is the old count struck through, right beside the new one. Both reviewers misread it, so it's a real readability failure even though the numbers are right.
  • Marcus's "Vaughn 5+ years" is a little off: the ledger has Vaughn at 4.142 years of service. The point holds for players at 5+ years, and the sheet does show option years for anyone under 5.
  • WAR comes from the app's war.json. I haven't confirmed which source's WAR it is, so the label in change 8 needs that checked first.
